    About APALINK

    Built for schools 🎓

    APALINK is a modern school notes library. Teachers publish by section & level. Students learn from their teachers only — searchable, downloadable, offline-capable, free for schools.

    Help

    How APALINK works

    How do I find notes for me?

    Your section & level are set at registration. The Library only shows notes matching both — search inside them.

    Who can publish?

    Only teachers (school password APADE@2026 at registration). Students cannot publish.

    How do I download?

    Tap Preview, then Download. Files up to 50 MB, shared via cloud.
